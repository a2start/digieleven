About the CITB Test

The CITB Test is a computer-based examination designed to verify essential health, safety, and environmental awareness on UK construction sites. Passing the test is generally an essential requirement when applying for a CSCS card.
Questions: 50 Multiple Choice
Duration: 45 Minutes
Format: Touchscreen Computer
Locations: Nationwide Test Centres

Which CITB Test Do You Need?

Ensure you select the appropriate test level matching your trade and card requirements:

1. Operatives Test

Tests basic knowledge across safety, health, and environment topics. Required for the CSCS Green Labourer card and certain trade applications.

2. Specialist Tests

Includes general health and safety questions plus specialist questions tailored to specific trades:

  • Supervisory (SUP)
  • Working at Height (WAH)
  • Demolition (DEM)
  • HVACR (Heating & Plumbing, Ductwork, Refrigeration, Services)
  • Plumbing or Gas (PLUM)
  • Highway Works (HIW)
  • Tunnelling (TUN)
  • Lifts and Escalators (LAE)

3. Managers and Professionals (MAP) Test

Designed for managers, directors, and technical personnel covering CDM regulations, health and safety management, and legal requirements.

Identification Required for Your CITB Test

Candidates must strictly comply with CITB's official identification requirements on the day of the test. Photocopies or digital screen images of ID are not accepted.

  • Original and in-date: ID must be an original document and valid (not expired).
  • Name Match: The name on your identification must exactly match the name provided during test booking.
  • Acceptable Primary ID: A valid Passport (any nationality) or a valid UK Photocard Driving Licence with photograph and signature.
  • Secondary ID: If you do not possess an acceptable Primary ID, you must present two valid Secondary forms of ID (one photo ID and one with signature) or an official CITB ID Waiver form approved before your test.
  • Admissions Notice: Candidates without acceptable identification will not be admitted by the test centre and the test fee will not be refunded.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a CITB test cost?

The official CITB test fee is £23.50. Construction Helps charges an additional booking and administration service fee of £21.50 (inc. VAT), bringing the total standard price to £45.00.

Can I reschedule my test?

Yes, provided you give at least 72 hours notice prior to your scheduled appointment. Rescheduling requests with less than 72 hours notice cannot be accommodated without forfeiting the test fee.

What identification do I need on test day?

You must present a valid, in-date original Primary ID (Passport or UK Photocard Driving Licence) with photo and signature matching your booking name, or two valid Secondary IDs in accordance with the official CITB ID policy.

How long is the test result valid?

CITB Test results are generally valid for 2 years from the date of passing when applying for a CSCS card or affiliated construction scheme card.
