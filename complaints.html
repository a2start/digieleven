Complaints Procedure

Independent Third-Party Booking Service

Construction Helps (operated by Construction Helps Ltd) provides an independent third-party booking and administrative support service. We are not part of, associated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of CITB or CSCS. If you have any concerns regarding our booking assistance, administrative charges, or customer service, please follow our complaints procedure below.

Our Commitment to You

At Construction Helps, we strive to deliver high-quality booking and application support for candidates and companies across the UK construction sector. If any aspect of our service does not meet your expectations, we welcome your feedback and are committed to resolving complaints promptly, fairly, and transparently.

How to Submit a Complaint

If you are dissatisfied with any service provided by Construction Helps, you can lodge a formal complaint via email or post:
  • Email: info@constructionhelps.com (Subject: Formal Complaint - [Your Booking Reference / Name])
  • Telephone: 0800 002 5614 (Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM)
  • Postal Address: Complaints Department, Construction Helps Ltd, 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2H 9JQ

Information to Include in Your Complaint

To help us investigate and resolve your issue as quickly as possible, please provide the following details:
  • Candidate's Full Name
  • Booking Reference Number / Transaction Reference (if applicable)
  • Personal Email Address and Contact Telephone Number used during booking
  • A clear description of your complaint, including dates and details of any previous communications with our team
  • Any supporting documentation or receipts
  • Your desired outcome or proposed resolution

Our Investigation & Response Timeframes

We handle all formal complaints in accordance with the following structured process:

Stage 1 — Acknowledgment

We will acknowledge receipt of your complaint in writing within 2 business days and provide a unique complaint reference.

Stage 2 — Investigation

A senior manager will thoroughly review the circumstances, booking records, call recordings (where applicable), and correspondence.

Stage 3 — Formal Response

We will provide a comprehensive written resolution within 5 to 10 business days outlining findings and any corrective actions or justified refund.

Escalation Procedure

If you are not satisfied with the initial response from our customer support team, you may request an escalation to the Operations Director by replying to the complaint response email. The Operations Director will conduct an independent secondary review and issue a final determination within 5 business days of escalation.

Distinction Between CITB/CSCS and Construction Helps

Please note that Construction Helps is an independent third-party administration service. We have no authority over test venue operations, test invigilation, examination questions, test scoring, candidate admissions on the day of the test, or the eligibility criteria and approval decisions made by CITB or CSCS. For issues strictly concerning official CITB test centres or official CSCS scheme policies, we will direct you to the appropriate official body.
