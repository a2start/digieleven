Which CITB Test Do I Need To Take?

Passing the appropriate CITB Test is a mandatory step when applying for a CSCS card. For example, candidates seeking the Green Labourer Card typically take the Operatives CITB Test, which evaluates knowledge of site safety, health, and environmental awareness. Revision materials and practice questions can help you prepare effectively before attending the test centre.
