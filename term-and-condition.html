3. Candidate Information & Accuracy

To book a CITB Test or process a CSCS card application, the candidate's genuine personal details must be supplied, including full legal name, date of birth, National Insurance number, residential address, postcode, personal email address, and personal phone number. The candidate's name provided during booking must exactly match the name displayed on their valid photo identification document.

Construction Helps accepts no liability for test refusal or card rejection resulting from inaccurate, false, or mismatched personal information provided by the candidate or their representative.

4. Test Day Identification Requirements

Candidates must bring acceptable, valid, in-date, original identification to the test centre on the day of the test. Photocopies, screenshots, or digital images are strictly not accepted.

  • Primary ID: A valid Passport or UK Photocard Driving Licence with photograph and signature.
  • Secondary ID / Waiver: If Primary ID is not held, two valid forms of Secondary ID (one with photo, one with signature) or an official CITB ID Waiver form must be presented.
  • Official ID Policy: Full requirements are set out in the CITB Test ID Policy.
  • Refusal of Admission: If a candidate arrives without acceptable ID or arrives later than 15 minutes prior to the test time, test centre staff will refuse admission and the test fee is non-refundable.

5. Rescheduling and Cancellation Policies

CITB Test Rescheduling / Cancellation: Under standard CITB rules, a minimum of 72 hours notice prior to the scheduled test date and time is required to cancel or reschedule a test appointment. If notice of at least 72 hours is provided, the test can be rescheduled or the £23.50 official test fee refunded. Requests made with less than 72 hours notice cannot be refunded or rescheduled under CITB policy.

Construction Helps Administration Fee: The Construction Helps administration and service fee covers booking coordination, customer support, and administrative processing. Once the booking administration has commenced, this service fee is non-refundable unless Construction Helps has failed to perform the agreed service.

CSCS Card Applications: CSCS card application assistance commences immediately upon order placement. If CSCS rejects an application due to missing or ineligible qualifications, Construction Helps will assist in resolving the discrepancy. Cancellation of card processing requests are subject to an administration deduction covering communications with the candidate and CSCS.

6. Retake Protection Package Terms

  • The £20.00 retake option is only available when purchased simultaneously with the original test booking.
  • The retake must be booked and taken within 90 days of the date of the original test attempt.
  • If the candidate passes the test on the first attempt, the retake fee is non-refundable as it represents an optional protection fee.
  • The retake package is non-transferable to other candidates or different test types.
